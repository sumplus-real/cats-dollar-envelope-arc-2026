Keep your way out.

A fixed task principal, visible fees for every signer, and a recovery page you can keep. If the service goes offline, withdraw the unaccepted amount directly from the contract.

Accepted fees are irreversible rights of the fixed payee. They do not prove delivery or quality. The contract limits task principal; signer Gas limits apply to this app, not other wallet activity.

One root. Two bounded leaves.

A accepts 0.002 USDC. B0 accepts 0.003. B1 keeps 0.004 Offered. The owner can recover the unaccepted 0.005, while the accepted 0.005 stays with its fixed payees. All service responses in this demonstration are synthetic fixtures.

Gas belongs on the same bill.

Each signer has a separate allowance. Prepared, pending and Unknown transactions retain their maximum fee. Confirmed receipts replace that reserve with gasUsed × effectiveGasPrice. Owner exit fees remain reserved.
